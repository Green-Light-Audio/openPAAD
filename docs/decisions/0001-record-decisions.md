# 0001. Record architecture decisions

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-02

## Context

openPAAD is built in the open by a small team that changes over time.
Reasons for choices must survive the people and the discussions that made
them.

## Decision

Every decision that would surprise a new contributor gets a short record in
`docs/decisions/NNNN-title.md`: context, decision, consequences. Records are
never rewritten; a later record supersedes an earlier one.

## Consequences

Reviews can point to a record instead of re-arguing. Superseded records stay
as history.
